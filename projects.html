NFC Business Card

Personal Project

To stand out at the career fair, I wanted to go beyond simply handing out paper résumés. I created custom NFC business cards that, when tapped, directed recruiters to my personal portfolio website--demonstrating both creativity and technical initiative. To give the cards a polished, professional look, I designed and printed custom graphics using my Cricut’s print-and-cut feature, then applied the vinyl designs to the cards. The result was a unique, interactive way to showcase my skills, which received enthusiastic feedback from recruiters.

Bloom Box

STL CAPS Project

As a student at STL CAPS, a key part of the curriculum focused on developing business professionalism through real-world experiences. One major opportunity came through a competition sponsored by SM Wilson, challenging students to design a STEAM-focused project (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art, and Math) for K–12 audiences. My team created the Bloom Box—a hands-on garden box that allowed students to design, engineer, build, decorate, and plant flowers, incorporating elements of science, engineering, art, and math. SM Wilson was highly impressed with our concept, and we were awarded the Skilled Centennial Awards Grand Prize. Following our win, our team was invited to present the project at the Missouri State Capitol to Governor Mike Parson—a truly unforgettable and inspiring experience.
